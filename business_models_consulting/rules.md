# Consulting Rules

## Evidence rules

- Do not invent facts, motives, governance structures, revenue models, or technical capabilities.
- Cite primary sources when available.
- Read the relevant folders under `case-study/` as primary material. That folder is gitignored, so ignore-respecting searches return nothing from it; open it directly or use `rg --no-ignore`, and never read a missed search as evidence of absence.
- Use Grounded Docs for OVN, P2P, Sensorica, contribution accounting, benefit redistribution, nondominium, and related concepts.
- Use current OVN vocabulary. "Fluid equity" and "value equation" are retired; use [fluid participation attributions](https://ovn.world/index.php?title=Fluid_participation_attributions), contribution-derived participation, contribution accounting, and benefit redistribution. When an assessed organization uses the retired terms, treat that as dated vocabulary rather than a different model.
- Separate `evidence`, `interpretation`, `hypothesis`, and `suggestion`.
- Mark missing evidence as missing evidence, not as proof of absence.
- Do not infer participant lived experience without participant narratives.

## Tone rules

- Write as an invitation, not a verdict.
- Treat current monetization as legitimate unless evidence shows deception or harm.
- Avoid accusing founders or teams of extraction when the better framing is structural risk.
- Avoid P2P purity tests.
- Prefer "opportunity", "transition", "path", "pattern", "capability", and "stewardship" over moralized language.
- Explain OVN and P2P terms as if the reader is new to them.

## Strategic framing rules

- Start from the organization's stated mission and actual capabilities.
- Show where current architecture already points beyond the current business model.
- Describe the current model before recommending alternatives.
- Link technical capabilities to economic possibilities.
- Explain why some value layers require ecosystem logic rather than ordinary transactions.
- Preserve the organization's ethical center while expanding opportunity.

## P2P / OVN usage rules

- Use P2P and OVN language only when it clarifies a practical design problem.
- When using `venture`, `benefit redistribution`, `contribution accounting`, `custodian`, or `nondominium`, add a short explanation and link where possible.
- Do not imply the whole company must convert into an OVN.
- Prefer edge experiments: scoped ventures, steward circles, infrastructure pools, contribution records, and shared standards.

## Human supervision rules

The human assessor must review:

- high-impact claims;
- tone;
- recommendations;
- gaps and uncertainty;
- whether the output could damage trust if read by the target organization;
- whether the proposal is actually useful to founders or stewards.

Record meaningful human input in `templates/human_supervision_log.md`.

## Unsolicited consulting ethics

When the work is unsolicited:

- be extra careful with claims about motives;
- do not speculate about internal finances beyond public evidence;
- frame risks as structural, not personal;
- avoid public shaming;
- write as if the founder might read the document in good faith;
- make the benefits of receiving the analysis obvious.

## Output quality rules

Every final consulting output should include:

- current position;
- current economic model;
- technical capability map;
- value-layer opportunity map;
- monetization or governance gates;
- sustainability models;
- migration path;
- founder-facing strategic message;
- key references.
