# Claims Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing consulting outputs.

## Claim register

| Claim | Source / evidence | Status | Confidence | Action |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
|  |  | Evidenced / Partially evidenced / Not evidenced / Contradicted / Hypothesis | High / Medium / Low | Keep / soften / remove / verify |

## Required checks

- [ ] Current economic model claims are supported.
- [ ] Technical capability claims are supported by docs, code, or primary sources.
- [ ] Governance claims distinguish absence of evidence from evidence of absence.
- [ ] Monetization claims do not assume bad intent.
- [ ] OVN / P2P claims are grounded in docs or repository foundations.
- [ ] Speculative opportunities are clearly marked as opportunities.
- [ ] Non-currency value claims are explained.
- [ ] Recommendations are feasible at the proposed stage.
- [ ] Human input has been logged.
- [ ] The final tone is helpful to the organization's stewards.

## Remove or revise if

- the claim depends on private motives;
- the claim sounds accusatory without evidence;
- the claim uses jargon without explanation;
- the claim overstates what the technology currently does;
- the recommendation requires a full organizational conversion too early.
