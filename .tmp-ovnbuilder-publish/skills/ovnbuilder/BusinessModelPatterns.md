## OVN adaptation rules (apply to every recommendation)

1. Preserve contribution accounting and multi-dimension benefits.
2. Do not enclose the commons / nondominium layer.
3. Prefer transparency; flag opaque capture (`use_with_caution`).
4. Infrastructure-agnostic — no mandatory platform.
5. Capital vs commons terms → HumanEscalation.

## Additional OVN-oriented patterns

### Commons Stewardship as a Service

Provide stewardship services for commons assets without claiming residual ownership.

- Adaptation: Custodian role separation; nondominium-compatible mandates.

### Protocol Cooperative

Cooperatively maintain protocols/interfaces that many ventures share.

- Adaptation: Open governance of protocol changes; infrastructure reciprocity.

### Outcome Commons Contracting

Contract for shared outcomes while logging contributions to commons deliverables.

- Adaptation: Transparent outcome metrics; avoid privatizing the commons result.

### Distributed Capability Incubation

Incubate capabilities across affiliates rather than concentrating IP in a single firm.

- Adaptation: Contribution-derived participation; portable reputation.
