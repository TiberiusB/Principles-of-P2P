## What / Problem / How

**What:** Portable skill for designing and operating Open Value Networks.

**Problem:** OVN doctrine and Sensorica praxis are scattered across wiki, Drive TEMPLATEs, and living ventures; AI agents invent doctrine or skip human judgment where complexity requires experience.

**How:** Route to workflows; ground answers in `Knowledge/` (ArgIndex + excerpts); remix Drive TEMPLATEs via `Templates/Catalog.md`; escalate to humans via `HumanEscalation.md` + `HelpHumans.md` when stakes require lived network experience.

## Source authority

Prefer Knowledge pack citations over parametric memory. Order: OVN wiki → living praxis (TEMPLATE / `(S)` / mature ventures) → sensorica.co → complementary blogs/p2p wiki → archived. See `SourceAuthority.md`.

Sensorica is a **historical precursor** and **living help destination**, not the required brand of the user’s network.
