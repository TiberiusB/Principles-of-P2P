# CollaborativeEntrepreneurship (context SOP)

## Fourth Sector framing

Collaborative entrepreneurship is the **Fourth Sector** mode of starting and sustaining open ventures (`ovn.fourth_sector`, `FourthSector.md`). The economy is **p2p economics**; the initiator type is the **collaborative entrepreneur** (not private-sector entrepreneur or solidarity social entrepreneur). See wiki incentives section: https://ovn.world/index.php?title=Fourth_sector
