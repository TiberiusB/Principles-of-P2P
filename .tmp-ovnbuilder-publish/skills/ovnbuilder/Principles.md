# Principles

Infrastructure-agnostic OVN invariants for OvnBuilder.

## Core

1. **Open / permissionless participation** — affiliation is not gated by employment or exclusive membership contracts as the default.
2. **Contribution-related benefits** — benefits (revenue, governance voice, reputation, access) relate to logged and evaluated contributions, not solely to capital ownership. Prefer “contribution-derived participation” over “fluid equity” as doctrine language.
3. **Transparency and stigmergy** — work leaves traces others can see and build on (horizontal and vertical visibility).
4. **Commons + custodianship** — shared resources under custodial / nondominium-class regimes; custodian ≠ owner-extractor.
5. **Role separation** — Custodian (property, liability, transactions, referee) vs Exchange firm (market interface) vs Affiliates vs Ventures.
6. **Infrastructure as pattern, not brand** — digital environment features first; tool examples carry caveats; no mandatory NRP product.
7. **Network of networks** — ventures nest under network norms; networks may federate.
8. **Disintermediation trajectory** — reduce capture points (platform lock-in, opaque allocation, plutocratic governance).
9. **Sector positioning** — OVN practice sits in the **Fourth Sector** (`ovn.fourth_sector`): p2p economics, nondominium-class property, collaborative entrepreneurship. Name **private**, **public**, and **solidarity** (3rd) sectors when contrasting. Do not subsume OVNs under social-economy or startup boxes by default; interfaces with other sectors (grants, exchange firms) are transfers, not identity collapse. See `FourthSector.md`.

## Sensorica framing

Sensorica is a **historical precursor** and a **living place of experience**. Cite practice and TEMPLATEs; do not require users to adopt the Sensorica brand. Point help-seekers to OVN support and trusted affiliates (`HelpHumans.md`).
