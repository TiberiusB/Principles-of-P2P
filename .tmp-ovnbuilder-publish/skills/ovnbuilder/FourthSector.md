# Fourth Sector (context)

Concept: `ovn.fourth_sector`  
Primary: https://ovn.world/index.php?title=Fourth_sector  
Secondary (history / legitimacy rationale): https://docs.google.com/document/d/1RPZzvrLx1yy3_HeTYk7dhxyRnNmIk0O6GRC8zRBqbf4/edit

## Why name it

The Fourth Sector names p2p, open, and collaborative socioeconomic activity that cannot be boxed as private, public, or solidarity (3rd) practice. Naming supports identity and delineation — property regime, governance, organizational architecture — and resists capture by bureaucratic or centralized processes. Introduce **private**, **public**, and **solidarity** when contrasting; introduce **Fourth Sector** whenever the activity sits on p2p / OVN axes.

The Fourth Sector is **orthogonal** to the other three, not a residual afterthought.

## Sector map

| Sector | Property (dominant) | Value language | Who starts things | Governance skew |
|--------|---------------------|----------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Public | public property | public value | bureaucrats / public managers | state / bureaucracy |
| Private | private property | market value | entrepreneurs | capital / firm |
| Solidarity (3rd) | shared property / commons (often state-protected) | social value | social entrepreneurs | membership / mission bureaucracy |
| Fourth | nondominium (`ovn.nondominium`) | multi-value / contribution-linked (p2p) | collaborative entrepreneurs | stigmergy + peer governance |

Economy of the Fourth Sector: **p2p economics** (`ovn.economic_model`). OVN practice is a primary organizational pattern inside this sector (`ovn.definition`).

## Interfaces without identity collapse

Fourth-sector networks may take grants, donations, or sell through an exchange firm. Those are **transfers / interfaces** with other sectors — not a reason to reclassify the network as a startup, NGO, or ministry project. Custodian (nondominium care) stays distinct from exchange firm (market interface).

## Vs solidarity (3rd) sector

Social-economy legal forms and models do not fully map to open network / p2p practice. Do not default-subsume an OVN under coop / nonprofit boxes. Escalate legal form choice (`HumanEscalation.md`).

## Sensorica framing

Sensorica proposed the term (May 2019 rationale, NOICE context) and remains a living help destination — not a required brand for the user’s network (`HelpHumans.md`).
