## Definitions

- **Contribution** vs **Donation** — contribution expects benefit-policy adherence; donation need not.
- **Cash contribution / donation** — monetary transfer.
- **Contributor / Donor / Sender**; **Receiver** (may be Custodian).

## Acknowledgement procedure (checklist)

1. Transfer occurred  
2. Intent declared (contribute vs donate)  
3. Venture or OVN context named  
4. Receiver awareness  
5. Logged in CAS / nondominium-class record  
6. Roles of contributor/recipient clear  
7. Signed agreement linking benefits (if contribution)

## Channel

Open, persistent acknowledgement channel (third-party persistent preferred) — feature-level; no mandated product.
